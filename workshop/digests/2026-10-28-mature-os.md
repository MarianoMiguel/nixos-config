# What mature desktops take care of (2026-10-28)

A survey of Apple (macOS/iOS 26 and the WWDC26 follow-ups), GNOME 49–51 and
current interaction design, read for Folio. Verified items cite a source;
the rest is inference. Ideas worth judging are on the taste board with
`from: research`.

## Behaviours a young desktop misses, by impact

1. **Interruptible springs everywhere.** Any animation can be retargeted
   mid-flight and keeps its velocity; curves cannot, springs can. Start with
   no bounce; add some only when the gesture had momentum (Apple Music: 100%
   damping on tap, 80% on swipe-dismiss); above 0.4 bounce looks silly [3][4].
   Match the shell's QML springs to niri's (damping 1.0, stiffness 1000) [21].
2. **Leave the way you came.** Exits mirror entries; sheets spring out of the
   control that opened them; GNOME's search overlay spawns from its entry
   [2][3][17]. For Folio: leafs leave by their spine, the island collapses
   into itself, a dismissed toast flies to the sidebar stack.
3. **Gestures 1:1 with rubber-banding and momentum projection** [3].
4. **Morph, don't rebuild.** Move existing elements to new positions; Live
   Activity transitions stay under 2 s [6]. The island's whole rule.
5. **Concentric radii.** Inner = outer − padding; three shape types: fixed,
   capsule, concentric [2]. Apple made it a WWDC26 keynote item [18][19].
6. **Privacy indicators.** Mic orange, camera green, one dot at a time, the
   responsible apps listed [10]. (Desktop v3 has a first pass.)
7. **Lock before sleep**, so resume never flashes the desktop.
8. **Floating screenshot thumbnail** to drag, mark up or dismiss [11]; arrow
   keys nudge the region (GNOME 51) [14].
9. **Interruption discipline.** Update only on new content, end activities
   when the task ends, never a notification and a live activity for the same
   event [6]; Do Not Disturb in quick settings (GNOME 49) [13].
10. **Undo, not "are you sure?"** Toasts with Undo after destructive actions [16].
11. **State restoration.** `xdg-session-management` landed in March 2026 [23];
    also keep the shell's own state (timers, sidebar) across restarts.
12. **Quick Look previews**, in files and in launcher results [14].
13. **Media on the lock screen** (GNOME 49) [13].
14. **Inactive things recede** when a window loses focus [1].
15. **Search that acts**: app actions, clipboard history, results with
    several actions and filters [12][15].
16. **Pointer finesse.** Magnetism only for highlight and lift effects, never
    hover; ~10 pt drag hysteresis [3][8].
17. **No dropped frames under load** (GNOME 51 frame scheduling) [14].
18. **Remembered hardware state**: brightness per monitor, "plugged in, not
    charging" [13][14].
19. **Optical centring**, not only mathematical [2].
20. **Restraint on frequent actions.** Do not animate what people do all day;
    never make them wait for motion [7]. Develop the first time, not every time.
21. **Sound design**, separate from alerts and optional. Paper-and-press foley
    would suit Folio.

## Trends, translated to ink

- **Materialize, don't fade**: Liquid Glass ramps its lensing instead of
  opacity, and pressed glass lights from within [1]. Ink: ramp the dither
  threshold; on press, ink pools under the control.
- **Merging shapes** [9]: wet-ink blobs that merge (smooth-min SDF) for the
  island-to-sidebar handoff and dock tooltips.
- **Scroll-edge effects** replace dividers under floating bars [2]: a
  stochastic dissolve into paper.
- **Adaptive contrast** follows what is underneath [1][15]: the bar's ink
  weight follows the plate's brightness.
- **Continuous corners**: CSS `corner-shape: superellipse(2)` [22]; in print,
  a plate mark with a concentric debossed line.
- **Shared-element transitions** that stay interactive [5]: a sidebar card
  grows into its window and closes back into it.
- **Live activities** with compact, minimal and expanded forms [6]: builds,
  downloads, timers in the island.
- **Lift on hover** [8]: a faint offset "ghost impression" under the control.
- **The clock in the picture's negative space** (iOS 26) [25]; paper warming
  at dusk.
- Dither and halftone are on trend in shader tools [26]; real print logic
  (registration, plate marks, ink spread) keeps it from dating.

## Avoid

- **Material on material**: a dithered panel over a dithered plate moirés.
  Keep panels on flat paper, or put every dither on one screen grid [1][24].
- Icons on every menu item, floating inset sidebars, directional highlights
  on app icons: shipped in macOS 26, rolled back in macOS 27 [19].
- Bounce above ~0.4, long or blocking motion [4][7].
- Sliders that let the design be dialled away instead of getting the default
  right [19].
- Dither that swims: anchor it to the object and snap motion to whole pixels.
- Effects without meaning: laptop parallax, AI sparkle, full-screen theme
  takeovers every time. Keep the bleed brief and interruptible.
